OUR FOUNDATION

Driving Sustainable Transformation for Future Generations

Desh Ki Pukaar Foundation is a social impact organization committed to positive change through initiatives in education, healthcare, environmental conservation, social welfare and disaster relief. We work with communities to promote opportunity, wellbeing and a more inclusive and resilient society.
